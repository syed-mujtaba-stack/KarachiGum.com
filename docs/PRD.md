# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Title

KarachiGum.com – Modern Web Revamp with AI Sales Assistant

## Prepared By

Intern Frontend / Full-Stack Developer

## Tech Stack

* Next.js (App Router)
* React
* Tailwind CSS
* shadcn/ui
* React Icons
* Next.js Server Components & Server Actions
* OpenRouter API (AI Chatbot)

---

## 1. Background & Problem Statement

The current KarachiGum.com website has an outdated UI, poor mobile responsiveness, weak content hierarchy, and no interactive or lead-generation features. This limits the company’s ability to attract international buyers, build trust, and convert visitors into export leads.

---

## 2. Product Vision

To build a modern, professional, and export-focused website that represents Karachi Gum as a global industrial chemical brand, enhanced with an AI-powered sales assistant to improve customer engagement and lead generation.

---

## 3. Goals & Objectives

### Business Goals

* Improve brand credibility for international markets
* Increase export inquiries and leads
* Provide 24/7 product assistance via AI chatbot
* Improve SEO and search visibility

### User Goals

* Easily understand products and applications
* Quickly find the right product for their industry
* Get instant answers about export, MOQ, and specs
* Contact sales with minimal friction

---

## 4. Target Users

### Primary Users

* International buyers & importers
* Industrial manufacturers
* Distributors & agents

### Secondary Users

* Internal sales team
* Potential partners

---

## 5. Key Features

### 5.1 Website Pages

#### Home Page

* Hero section with clear value proposition
* Primary CTAs: **Get a Quote** & **Talk to AI Assistant**
* Product category overview
* Why Choose Us section
* Export & global presence section
* Footer with contact information

#### Products

* Product listing by category
* Individual product detail pages
* Technical specifications table
* Applications & usage
* Packaging & export info
* CTA: *Ask AI about this product*

#### About Us

* Company introduction
* Manufacturing process
* Mission & vision
* Quality assurance

#### Industries We Serve

* Industry-wise cards
* Linked recommended products

#### Contact / Get a Quote

* Modern form (Name, Company, Country, Product, Message)
* Server-side form handling

---

### 5.2 AI Chatbot (Core Feature)

#### Purpose

* Act as a virtual sales & product assistant
* Answer product, export, and industry questions
* Guide users toward contacting sales

#### Capabilities

* Product explanation
* Industry-based product recommendations
* MOQ & packaging info
* Export availability guidance

#### Limitations

* Only answer Karachi Gum related queries
* No unrelated/general questions

---

## 6. Functional Requirements

* Server-side rendering for SEO
* Fully responsive design (mobile, tablet, desktop)
* Fast load times & optimized images
* AI chatbot available across all pages
* Secure server actions for forms

---

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

* Performance score 90+
* SEO-friendly structure
* Clean, maintainable codebase
* Scalable architecture for future admin panel

---

## 8. UI/UX Requirements

### Design Style

* Industrial & professional
* Clean layout with strong typography
* Trust-focused visuals

### Color Palette

* Primary: Dark Green
* Secondary: Industrial Gray
* Accent: Gold / Amber

### Components

* Cards, Tables, Dialogs, Accordions
* Consistent spacing and grid system

## 9. SEO & Analytics

## 10. Future Enhancements

* Content accuracy → Manual review

## 14. Conclusion

This revamp will transform KarachiGum.com into a modern, AI-enabled, export-ready platform that strengthens brand trust, improves customer experience, and increases business opportunities.
